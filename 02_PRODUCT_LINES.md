# SYDTEK LEARNING PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE
## 02 — PRODUCT LINES

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** CANONICAL — LOCKED FOR INITIAL BUILD

---

# PURPOSE

This document defines the canonical SydTek product architecture.

It establishes:

- the official product families
- target users
- product purpose
- monetization role
- academic relationship
- progression between products
- which products are current, emerging, or future
- how each product contributes to the larger SydTek platform

The goal is to prevent SydTek from becoming an unstructured collection of courses, communities, tools, and experiments.

---

# PRODUCT NORTH STAR

SydTek products should move people through a coherent progression:

**DISCOVER  
→ LEARN  
→ PRACTICE  
→ BUILD  
→ PROVE  
→ CREDENTIAL  
→ STACK  
→ APPLY  
→ RETURN**

The product portfolio should support that progression without forcing every learner into the same path.

---

# CANONICAL PRODUCT FAMILY

The canonical SydTek product family consists of:

1. **SydTek Free**
2. **SydTek Standard**
3. **SydTek Plus**
4. **SydTek Credentials**
5. **SydTek Labs**
6. **SydTek Campus**
7. **SydTek Enterprise**

These names define product categories.

Individual courses, certificates, laboratories, and institutional offerings sit beneath them.

# 1. SYDTEK FREE

## PURPOSE

SydTek Free is the public-access learning layer.

Its job is to:

- attract learners
- establish trust
- provide meaningful educational value
- introduce SydTek learning methods
- create the first measurable learning event
- move learners toward deeper study

SydTek Free should not be treated as a weak teaser.

It should be genuinely useful.

---

# TARGET USERS

- beginners
- curious learners
- prospective students
- career changers
- professionals exploring a topic
- families
- community learners
- people entering through YouTube, social media, search, AI, or referrals

---

SydTek Free may include:

- free courses
- introductory modules
- public webinars
- public videos
- open educational materials
- downloadable worksheets
- public challenges
- orientation programs
- community learning

# 2. SYDTEK STANDARD

## PURPOSE

SydTek Standard is the core individual-course product.

Current default:

# $29 PER SELF-PACED SPECIALIZED COURSE

The $29 standard creates a simple transition between free access and paid specialized education.

---

# TARGET USERS

- individual learners
- working professionals
- career changers
- entrepreneurs
- creators
- independent learners
- students who want one specific skill

---

# PRODUCT FORMAT

A SydTek Standard course should normally contain:

- course description
- learning outcomes
- modules
- lessons
- outputs
- assignments
- artifacts
- capstone where appropriate
- completion requirements
- next-course recommendation

---

# ROLE IN THE BUSINESS

SydTek Standard is designed to accomplish three things:

## FIRST PURCHASE

Convert a free learner into a paying learner.

## SPECIALIZATION

Allow learners to study a focused subject without committing to a large program.

## STACKING

Allow multiple courses to accumulate toward credentials.

---

# STANDARD COURSE RULE

A $29 course should not exist merely because content exists.

It should teach a coherent skill or capability.

Recommended test:

> Can the learner explain what they can do after completing this course that they could not reliably do before?

If not, the product may be better classified as content rather than a course.

# 3. SYDTEK PLUS

## PURPOSE

SydTek Plus is the future consumer subscription layer.

It is designed for learners who want ongoing access rather than repeated individual purchases.

---

# TARGET USERS

- professionals reskilling continuously
- high-engagement community members

**Annual subscription**

- learning pathways

or

**SUBSCRIBE TO THE NETWORK**

# PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT RULE

Do not fully build every product simultaneously.

Products should advance based on:

**DEMAND  
+ EVIDENCE  
+ STRATEGIC NEED  
+ OPERATING CAPACITY**

---

# PRODUCT SUNSET RULE

A product may be:

- merged
- renamed
- replaced
- archived
- sunset

without destroying the underlying:

- course records
- learner records
- credentials
- artifacts
- contracts
- version history

The product label may change.

The institutional record must persist.

---

# CHANNEL INDEPENDENCE

Products may be delivered through:

- Skool
- SydTek-owned software
- YouTube
- institutional LMS
- employer LMS
- email
- live cohorts
- mobile platforms
- partner systems

No single third-party platform should become the only canonical record of a SydTek product.

---

# MONETIZATION PRINCIPLE

Not every product must directly generate revenue.

Example:

**SydTek Free**

may create value by producing:

- registrations
- audience
- trust
- learner data
- referrals
- conversions
- institutional visibility

The portfolio should be evaluated as a system.

---

# ACADEMIC FIREWALL

Commercial incentives should not determine:

- grades
- mastery
- credential issuance
- assessment results
- academic integrity decisions

Revenue and academic evaluation must remain distinguishable.

This is especially important where SydTek may receive:

- referral compensation
- affiliate fees
- platform fees
- sponsor funding
- trading-related revenue
- employer funding

---

# PRODUCT SUCCESS MODEL

SydTek should evaluate each product through:

**ACQUISITION  
→ ACTIVATION  
→ LEARNING  
→ RETENTION  
→ MONETIZATION  
→ OUTCOME  
→ EXPANSION**

No product should be judged solely by revenue.
