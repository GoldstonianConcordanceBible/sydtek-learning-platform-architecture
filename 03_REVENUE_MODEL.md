**Version:** 1.0

# PURPOSE

This document defines how SydTek generates revenue while preserving:

- academic integrity
- learner trust
- institutional clarity
- financial separation
- auditability
- scalability
- public-company readiness

SydTek should not depend on a single revenue source.

The long-term model is diversified across:

- consumer education
- subscriptions
- credentials
- institutional licensing
- enterprise training
- government/workforce programs
- labs and sponsored challenges
- platform licensing
- AI services
- research
- selected referral and affiliate activity

---

# REVENUE PRINCIPLE

## EDUCATION IS THE CORE PRODUCT.

Secondary commercial activity may support the ecosystem, but it must not distort academic evaluation.

The central business relationship is:

**LEARNER / ORGANIZATION  
→ EDUCATIONAL VALUE  
→ PAYMENT  
→ VERIFIED CAPABILITY**

---

# REVENUE NORTH STAR

SydTek should progress from:

**ONE-TIME COURSE SALES**

toward:

**RECURRING + INSTITUTIONAL + PLATFORM REVENUE**

The long-term target is not merely to sell more $29 courses.

The objective is to build a business with:

- recurring revenue
- repeat purchases
- high learner retention
- long-duration institutional contracts
- enterprise renewals
- scalable gross margins
- licensable infrastructure

---

---

# ACADEMIC FIREWALL

The learner must never be required to:

- use a referral link
- fund an account
- purchase an external product
- trade
- use leverage
- copy trade

to earn academic completion unless the underlying action is itself a legitimate non-commercial technical requirement and an equivalent non-compensated route exists.

---

# FINANCIAL EDUCATION FIREWALL

Particularly for market courses:

## EDUCATIONAL SUCCESS ≠ TRADING PROFIT.

Student evaluation should measure:

- analysis
- thesis quality
- risk controls
- documentation
- evidence
- process
- reflection
- governance

not merely:

- profit
- trade frequency
- deposit size
- referral volume

# WHY SEPARATION MATTERS

Revenue separation enables:

- product profitability analysis
- investor diligence
- institutional review
- conflict review
- tax reporting
- strategic decisions
- customer concentration analysis

---

# TREASURY SEPARATION

Where applicable, maintain operational separation between:

**EDUCATION TREASURY**

and:

**TRADING / DIGITAL-ASSET / EXPERIMENTAL TREASURY**

Do not treat speculative gains as ordinary tuition revenue.

Do not use learner deposits or academic funds as trading capital without explicit lawful structure and authorization.

---

# REVENUE RECOGNITION DISCIPLINE

As the company grows, revenue accounting must distinguish:

- immediate course sales
- subscription periods
- deferred revenue
- prepaid licenses
- institutional contracts
- multi-year contracts
- grant revenue
- sponsorship
- contingent compensation

Formal accounting policy should be developed with qualified accounting professionals as complexity increases.

# UNIT ECONOMICS

At minimum, SydTek should eventually know:

## CONSUMER

**CAC**

Customer acquisition cost.

**LTV**

Lifetime value.

**AOV**

Average order value.

**PAYBACK PERIOD**

How long acquisition spend takes to recover.

---

## SUBSCRIPTION

**MRR**

Monthly recurring revenue.

**ARR**

Annual recurring revenue.

**CHURN**

Lost customers or revenue.

**LTV:CAC**

Relationship between customer value and acquisition cost.

---

## INSTITUTIONAL

**ACV**

Annual contract value.

**SALES CYCLE**

Time from lead to signed agreement.

**IMPLEMENTATION COST**

Cost to activate customer.

**RENEWAL RATE**

Contract renewal.

**NRR**

Net revenue retention after churn and expansion.

# PUBLIC-COMPANY REVENUE CHARACTERISTICS

Before serious public-market consideration, SydTek should seek:

- significant recurring revenue
- strong retention
- diversified customer base
- predictable renewals
- scalable gross margins
- low dependence on one founder
- low dependence on one customer
- transparent revenue recognition
- audited financials

---

# REVENUE QUALITY HIERARCHY

Not all revenue has equal strategic quality.

Long-duration recurring contracts may be more valuable than one-time transactions.

A useful hierarchy:

**ONE-TIME TRANSACTION**

↓

**REPEAT CUSTOMER**

↓

**SUBSCRIPTION**

↓

**RECURRING INSTITUTIONAL CONTRACT**

↓

**MULTI-PRODUCT EXPANSION**

↓

**PLATFORM DEPENDENCE / DEEP INTEGRATION**

The objective is to move more revenue toward durable relationships.

Pricing changes should be recorded.

- product
- old price
- new price
- date
- rationale
- cohort affected
- grandfathering policy

Do not silently change institutional contracts or learner promises.

# DISCOUNT GOVERNANCE

Discounts should be deliberate.

Possible categories:

- launch promotion
- scholarship
- partner discount
- cohort discount
- institutional volume discount
- employee benefit
- community access

Track discounting so headline revenue is not confused with net realized revenue.

# SCHOLARSHIPS

SydTek may provide:

- full scholarships
- partial scholarships
- sponsored seats
- need-based access
- community access

# REVENUE & ACADEMIC INDEPENDENCE

Commercial teams may influence:

- product demand analysis
- pricing
- customer needs
- product packaging

- academic findings
- research results

# CONFLICT-OF-INTEREST DISCLOSURE

Where a course discusses a company, platform, product, market, or partner from which SydTek receives compensation, disclose the relationship where material.

This protects:

- learner trust
- institutional integrity
- research credibility
- long-term brand value

**REVENUE INTEREST**

# CREATE VALUE → VERIFY VALUE → PRICE VALUE → RETAIN TRUST.
