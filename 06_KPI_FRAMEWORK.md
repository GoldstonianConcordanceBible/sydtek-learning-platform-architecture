# SYDTEK LEARNING PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE
## 06 — KPI FRAMEWORK

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** CANONICAL — LOCKED FOR INITIAL BUILD

---

# PURPOSE

This document defines the canonical SydTek KPI framework.

It establishes how SydTek measures:

- learner acquisition
- learner activation
- learning
- completion
- demonstrated capability
- monetization
- retention
- subscriptions
- credentials
- content utilization
- institutional sales
- enterprise sales
- product quality
- financial performance
- platform scale
- research activity
- operational risk
- public-company readiness

The objective is to prevent SydTek from using vanity metrics as substitutes for actual performance.

---

# KPI NORTH STAR

The primary question is not:

> How many courses do we have?

The stronger questions are:

> How many learners are active?

> What are they learning?

> What have they demonstrated?

> Are they returning?

> Are individuals and organizations paying?

> Are customers renewing?

> Is the business economically scalable?

---

# PRIMARY SYDTEK OUTCOME

The institutional north star is:

# VERIFIED HUMAN CAPABILITY CREATED

This should eventually be supported by measurable evidence such as:

- competencies demonstrated
- artifacts completed
- credentials earned
- hours saved
- workflows built
- projects completed
- research produced
- applied outcomes
- institutional acceptance
- employer use

# 1. ACQUISITION KPIs

Acquisition measures how people enter SydTek.

Track:

- website visitors
- landing-page visitors
- community visitors
- free-course visitors
- registered learners
- acquisition source
- campaign source
- referral source
- organic search traffic
- social traffic
- YouTube traffic
- partner traffic
- university traffic
- employer traffic
- direct traffic

---

# REGISTRATION RATE

Formula:

**REGISTERED LEARNERS  
÷ UNIQUE VISITORS**

Purpose:

Measures whether discovery becomes learner identity.

---

# COST PER REGISTRATION

Formula:

**ACQUISITION SPEND  
÷ NEW REGISTERED LEARNERS**

Use separately for:

- paid social
- search
- influencer
- event
- partnership
- referral campaigns

---

# ORGANIC SHARE

Formula:

**ORGANIC REGISTRATIONS  
÷ TOTAL REGISTRATIONS**

A strong organic share can indicate:

- useful public content
- strong word of mouth
- search visibility
- brand strength

---

# CONTENT-TO-LEARNER CONVERSION

For each major content asset:

**NEW LEARNERS ATTRIBUTED TO CONTENT  
÷ CONTENT VIEWERS / VISITORS**

This helps identify which videos, articles, posts, and case studies actually create learners.

---

# FOUNDING 1,000 KPI

Initial milestone:

# 1,000 REGISTERED LEARNERS

Supporting metrics:

- number started a free course
- number completed Lesson 1
- number completed one artifact
- number completed a full free course
- number purchased a paid course

# 2. ACTIVATION KPIs

Registration alone is not success.

Activation measures whether a registered learner begins meaningful learning.

---

# PRIMARY ACTIVATION EVENT

Recommended initial definition:

## FIRST LESSON COMPLETED

Stronger later definition:

## FIRST LEARNING OUTPUT COMPLETED

---

# ACTIVATION RATE

Formula:

**ACTIVATED LEARNERS  
÷ REGISTERED LEARNERS**

Track within:

- 24 hours
- 7 days
- 30 days

---

# START HERE COMPLETION RATE

Formula:

**LEARNERS COMPLETING START HERE  
÷ COURSE ENROLLMENTS**

This identifies onboarding quality.

---

# TIME TO FIRST LEARNING EVENT

Measure:

**REGISTRATION TIMESTAMP  
→ FIRST LESSON / OUTPUT TIMESTAMP**

Lower friction should generally reduce this interval.

---

# FIRST ARTIFACT RATE

Formula:

**LEARNERS COMPLETING FIRST ARTIFACT  
÷ ACTIVATED LEARNERS**

This is one of SydTek's most important early-learning metrics.

# 4. LEARNING KPIs

Learning metrics measure knowledge and capability development.

Track:

- assessment scores
- pre/post assessment improvement
- skill state
- competency state
- retry rate
- mastery rate
- rubric performance
- time to demonstrated competency

---

# PRE / POST IMPROVEMENT

Where useful:

**POST-ASSESSMENT SCORE  
− PRE-ASSESSMENT SCORE**

This may indicate learning gain.

---

# SKILL PROGRESSION

Recommended skill states:

- NOT STARTED
- INTRODUCED
- PRACTICED
- DEMONSTRATED
- MASTERED
- REVIEW DUE

Track the number and percentage of learners progressing through each state.

---

# COMPETENCY DEMONSTRATION RATE

Formula:

**LEARNERS WHO DEMONSTRATE COMPETENCY  
÷ LEARNERS ATTEMPTING COMPETENCY**

---

# MASTERY RATE

Formula:

**LEARNERS REACHING DEFINED MASTERY THRESHOLD  
÷ ELIGIBLE LEARNERS**

Mastery thresholds must be predefined.

---

# ASSESSMENT RETRY RATE

Formula:

**LEARNERS REQUIRING ADDITIONAL ATTEMPTS  
÷ LEARNERS ATTEMPTING ASSESSMENT**

High retry rates may indicate:

- difficult material
- weak instruction
- unclear assessment
- healthy learning rigor

Interpret with qualitative evidence.

- applied outcomes

# ARTIFACT COMPLETION RATE

Formula:

**REQUIRED ARTIFACTS COMPLETED  
÷ REQUIRED ARTIFACT OPPORTUNITIES**

---

# VERIFIED ARTIFACT RATE

Formula:

**ARTIFACTS REVIEWED / VERIFIED  
÷ ARTIFACTS SUBMITTED**

---

# PROOF PORTFOLIO RATE

Formula:

**LEARNERS WITH AT LEAST ONE VERIFIED PORTFOLIO ARTIFACT  
÷ ACTIVE LEARNERS**

---

# CAPABILITY DENSITY

Potential future measure:

**VERIFIED COMPETENCIES  
÷ ACTIVE LEARNER**

This can show how much demonstrated capability the platform produces.

---

# APPLIED OUTCOME RATE

Where measurable:

**LEARNERS REPORTING OR VERIFYING REAL APPLICATION  
÷ COMPLETERS**

Examples:

- used AI workflow at work
- completed business process redesign
- published a project
- submitted a grant
- built a portfolio
- launched a product
- completed a market analysis

---

# HOURS SAVED

For relevant AI and workflow programs:

Track:

- self-reported hours saved
- verified hours saved
- estimated annualized time savings

Use clearly defined methodology.

Do not inflate self-reported numbers into audited outcomes.

---

# SEAT UTILIZATION

Formula:

**ACTIVE SEATS  
÷ LICENSED SEATS**

# RENEWAL RATE

Formula:

**CUSTOMERS RENEWED  
÷ CUSTOMERS ELIGIBLE TO RENEW**

# NET REVENUE RETENTION

Conceptually:

**BEGINNING RECURRING REVENUE  
+ EXPANSION  
− CONTRACTION  
− CHURN  
÷ BEGINNING RECURRING REVENUE**

Track for institutional and enterprise customers when recurring contracts become material.

---

Track percentage of total revenue attributable to:

- largest customer

# GROSS MARGIN

Formula:

**REVENUE  
− COST OF REVENUE  
÷ REVENUE**

Track separately by:

- consumer
- Campus
- Enterprise
- Labs
- Platform

Different businesses may have different economics.

---

# CUSTOMER ACQUISITION COST

Formula:

**SALES + MARKETING COST ATTRIBUTABLE TO ACQUISITION  
÷ NEW CUSTOMERS**

Use cohort-based analysis where possible.

---

# LIFETIME VALUE

LTV methodology should be documented.

Do not publish a simplistic LTV number without defining assumptions.

Possible inputs include:

- average revenue
- gross margin
- retention
- customer lifetime

---

# LTV:CAC

Useful when both LTV and CAC are stable enough to be meaningful.

Early-stage estimates should be labeled as estimates.

---

# CASH RUNWAY

Formula:

**AVAILABLE CASH  
÷ AVERAGE MONTHLY NET CASH BURN**

If cash flow positive, runway is less relevant than cash generation.

---

# RECURRING REVENUE SHARE

Formula:

**RECURRING REVENUE  
÷ TOTAL REVENUE**

Track as the business matures.

---

# REVENUE MIX

# KPI OWNER RULE

Every major KPI should have:

- metric name
- definition
- formula
- data source
- owner
- reporting cadence
- review date

A metric without a definition will eventually become inconsistent.

---

# KPI DICTIONARY

Maintain a machine-readable KPI dictionary.

Example:

```yaml
metric_id: KPI-LEARN-001
name: Course Completion Rate
definition: Percentage of learners who started a course and completed all required course completion requirements.
numerator: Learners completing requirements
denominator: Learners who started course
owner: Academic Operations
cadence: Weekly
